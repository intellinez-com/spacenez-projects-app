Project payment tracking — 2026-11-21

Setup and use
- Open a project > Payments. An admin enters the agreed INR project total, scope,
  payment instructions and milestone schedule, then explicitly saves and shares it.
  Existing projects start unconfigured. No RCF amounts or payment history are seeded.
- Enter the final agreed total, including any applicable charges, as one amount.
  This module does not calculate tax or issue statutory tax invoices.
- Milestone due dates and explicit due markers determine what is due now. A written
  due condition explains the agreement; task changes and design approvals do not
  automatically demand payment. The remaining contract balance can include later work.
- Record an already-received advance/payment with its actual date and method.
  Allocate it to milestones or retain it as unallocated credit against the total.
  Partial payments are supported; an advance is part of the project price.
- A client can report a payment and attach a private proof. Reports remain pending
  and do not reduce outstanding until an admin checks and confirms receipt.
- Admins confirm/reject reports, correct records through a reasoned void, and record
  money already refunded against its original receipt. Recording a refund does not
  transfer money. The original receipt and the correction history are retained.
- This release tracks collections. It does not create online checkout sessions,
  charge cards/UPI, initiate refunds or start recurring subscriptions.

Access and evidence
- Database RPCs enforce admin-only setup, confirmation, allocation and corrections.
  Ordinary project editing permission does not confer finance editing permission.
- Assigned clients can read their project finances and submit pending receipts only.
  Partners have no finance access by default. Admins can explicitly share a redacted
  summary and milestones; references, payment records, proof files and audit remain
  unavailable to partners. Unassigned accounts and anonymous requests are denied.
- Financial changes have a separate immutable audit, not general project_activity.
  Daily status continues to describe project work without exposing financial details.
- Private payment proofs use project-finance/<project_id>/<uploader_id>/<random-file>.
  PDF, PNG, JPEG and WebP are allowed up to 5 MiB. Access uses short-lived signed URLs.
  Proofs already linked to payment history are retained through rejection/voiding.
- Money uses integer paise; updates and allocations are atomic and project-scoped.
  Stable payment IDs make request retries duplicate-safe. Quote version checks
  prevent an outdated admin editor from overwriting a newer agreement.
